# Comprehensive Publishing Guide for War Strike Mobile

## Overview
This guide provides detailed steps and considerations for publishing War Strike Mobile across various platforms: iOS App Store, Google Play Store, Steam, Epic Games Store, Xbox, and PlayStation.

---

## 1. iOS App Store
### Requirements:
- Apple Developer Account ($99/year)
- Xcode installed
- iOS 10.0 or later compatibility

### Steps:
1. **Setup Your App in App Store Connect**  
   - Create a new app record and fill in the necessary info.
2. **Pre-Launch Checklist**:
   - Make sure your app meets Apple's App Store Review Guidelines.
   - Ensure your app icons and screenshots are prepared.
3. **Test Your App**  
   - Use TestFlight for beta testing.  
4. **Submit for Review**  
   - Upload build through Xcode.
5. **Publishing**  
   - The app will undergo review, and you will get notified of approval/denial.

### Timelines:
- Preparation: 2-4 weeks
- Review time: 1-2 weeks

### Costs:
- $99/year for Apple Developer Program.

---

## 2. Google Play Store
### Requirements:
- Google Play Developer Account ($25 one-time fee)
- Target Android API Level (29+ recommended)

### Steps:
1. **Setup Your App in Google Play Console**  
   - Create a new app listing.
2. **Pre-Launch Checklist**:
   - Fill out app details including description, screenshots, and policies.
3. **Test Your App**  
   - Use internal testing or closed testing tracks.
4. **Submit for Review**  
   - Upload APK/App Bundle and rollout to production.

### Timelines:
- Preparation: 2-3 weeks
- Review time: 3-5 days

### Costs:
- $25 one-time fee for Google Play Developer Account.

---

## 3. Steam
### Requirements:
- Steamworks Account (Free)
- Steam Direct Fee ($100 per game)

### Steps:
1. **Create Steamworks Account**.
2. **Set Up Your Game**  
   - Fill out game details, upload builds, and set pricing.
3. **Testing**  
   - Use Steam's beta features for testing.
4. **Review and Release**  
   - Submit your game for review by Valve.

### Timelines:
- Preparation: 1-2 months
- Review time: 1-2 weeks

### Costs:
- $100 Steam Direct fee per game.

---

## 4. Epic Games Store
### Requirements:
- Epic Developer Account  

### Steps:
1. **Apply for a Publisher Account**.
2. **Set Up Your Game**  
   - Complete required forms and upload your game build.
3. **Testing Requirements**  
   - Utilize Epic's testing guidelines.
4. **Review and Release**  
   - Your game will be reviewed before going live.

### Timelines:
- Preparation: 1-2 months
- Review time: 2-3 weeks

### Costs:
- No upfront fees, but a revenue share applies.

---

## 5. Xbox
### Requirements:
- Xbox Developer Account  

### Steps:
1. **Create a Developer Account**  
   - Apply for a developer account.
2. **Set Up Your Game**  
   - Complete submission forms on Partner Center.
3. **Testing Requirements**  
   - Utilize Xbox's testing requirements.
4. **Submit for Certification**  
   - Your game goes through Xbox's certification process.

### Timelines:
- Preparation: 1-3 months
- Certification time: 2-4 weeks

### Costs:
- Varies based on developer program.

---

## 6. PlayStation
### Requirements:
- PlayStation Developer Account  

### Steps:
1. **Apply for a Developer Account**  
   - Register and get approved.
2. **Set Up Your Game**  
   - Submit your game for review in PlayStation's system.
3. **Testing Requirements**  
   - Follow PlayStation's testing guidelines.
4. **Submit for Certification**  
   - Your game is reviewed by PlayStation Certification.

### Timelines:
- Preparation: 1-3 months
- Certification time: 2-6 weeks

### Costs:
- Specific details vary based on agreements.

---

## Conclusion
This publishing guide outlines key requirements and steps to take when publishing War Strike Mobile across various platforms. Be sure to keep track of each platform's changes in requirements or processes to ensure a smooth publishing experience.
